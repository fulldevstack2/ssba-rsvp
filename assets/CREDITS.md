# Image credits

No photograph of Syed Saddiq or Bella Astillah is used anywhere on these sites.
Official photography should come from the couple's appointed photographer via
their team. Nothing on Wikimedia Commons is usable: there is one public-domain
diplomatic portrait of the groom, in a suit, and nothing at all of the bride.
Everything else online is someone's copyright, and an unlicensed image on an
invitation that circulates on WhatsApp is a risk not worth taking.

Drop official images in via the `--img-1` through `--img-4` custom properties
described in the README. The build inlines anything in this folder referenced as
`{{asset:filename}}`, so each design stays a single droppable file.

## A note on the bride's tradition, recorded so it is not got wrong later

The bride is Sabah, Brunei-Kedayan. **Iban pua kumbu was considered and
rejected**: Iban is Sarawak, a different state and a different people, and using
it here would read as "East Malaysia, close enough" to exactly the guests who
would notice.

Songket is the shared Malay-Nusantara ceremonial cloth on both sides of this
marriage. Brunei *jong sarat* is the same craft, supplementary-weft gold thread
on silk. If a woven motif is ever wanted, that is the accurate reference, and
no Kedayan or Brunei textile is available on Commons under a usable licence.
