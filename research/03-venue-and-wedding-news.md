## PART B — THE VENUE: MERDEKA 118 / MENARA MERDEKA MAYBANK

### B1. Name (VERIFIED)
- Built as Menara Warisan Merdeka / KL118 / PNB 118, opened as **Merdeka 118 (Menara Merdeka 118)**. Owner/developer: PNB Merdeka Ventures Sdn Bhd (Permodalan Nasional Berhad). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merdeka_118
- **Maybank is anchor tenant, not owner** (lease 12 Sep 2022; 33 floors; naming and signage rights). https://www.maybank.com/en/news/2022/09/12.page
- **Renamed "Menara Merdeka Maybank" — announced 28 Jul 2026.** Maybank HQ: Level 70, Menara Merdeka Maybank, Presint Merdeka 118, 50118 Kuala Lumpur. Precinct still branded Merdeka 118. https://www.thestar.com.my/business/business-news/2026/07/28/menara-merdeka-118-to-be-renamed-menara-merdeka-maybank ; https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-30/maybank-makes-world-s-second-tallest-tower-its-new-headquarters

### B2. Key facts (VERIFIED unless marked)
- **Height 678.9 m**; 118 storeys + 5 basements; **world's second-tallest building after Burj Khalifa; tallest in Southeast Asia.** Cost ~RM5 billion. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merdeka_118 ; https://news.samsungcnt.com/en/features/engineering-construction/2024-09-merdeka-118-an-engineering-marvel-and-the-worlds-second-tallest-building/
- **Spire ~160 m. Inspiration: Tunku Abdul Rahman's raised right hand as he proclaimed "Merdeka!" at Stadium Merdeka on 31 Aug 1957.** https://www.thorntontomasetti.com/project/merdeka-118
- **Facade:** faceted diamond/triangular glass panels **inspired by traditional Malay songket motifs**, symbolising the nation's diversity. https://www.fkaustralia.com/projects/merdeka-118 ; https://www.worldconstructionnetwork.com/projects/merdeka-118-kuala-lumpur-malaysia/
- Design: Fender Katsalidis with RSP KL; structure LERA; contractor Samsung C&T–UEM JV. Officially opened 10 Jan 2024 by the Yang di-Pertuan Agong Al-Sultan Abdullah. https://www.sarawaktribune.com/king-opens-menara-merdeka-118/
- **Location:** Jalan Hang Jebat, Presint Merdeka 118, 50118 Kuala Lumpur, adjacent to Stadium Merdeka and Stadium Negara; near Petaling Street.

### B3. What is where — and what is on Level 118
- Basements: parking; **Belfield Tunnel** (opened Mar 2025) from Jalan Syed Putra and Jalan Damansara directly into Basement 4; ~8,000 bays precinct-wide. https://paultan.org/2025/03/05/belfield-tunnel-opens-for-direct-access-to-merdeka-118-park-hyatt-opening-in-q2-2025-118-mall-in-q2-2026/
- **118 Mall** at the base (7 storeys, 300+ outlets; The Atmosphere event space L4). Opening 2026 (Aug/Q3/Nov per source); actual opening unverified. https://www.thestar.com.my/business/business-news/2026/05/05/merdeka-118-mall-set-for-august-opening-to-feature-300-retail-outlets
- Offices: Maybank mid-zone (~L42–74); PNB L91–96.
- **Park Hyatt Kuala Lumpur — Levels 75–114** (opened 7 Aug 2025; 252 rooms). Lobby and F&B on **L75** (Merdeka Grill, Park Lounge, Cacao Mixology & Chocolate); Executive Salons L98; spa/pool L99; Sky Rooms L114. https://newsroom.hyatt.com/080725-Park-Hyatt-Kuala-Lumpur-Opens-In-Malaysia ; https://www.hyatt.com/park-hyatt/en-US/kulph-park-hyatt-kuala-lumpur/weddings

L116 360° deck; L116M glass floor/Edge Walk; L117 conference centre, VIP lounge, luxury restaurant; **L118 upper indoor observatory at ~519 m** (Wikipedia) / "VIP lounge on the 118th floor" (Thornton Tomasetti). **Conclusion:** Level 118 is the topmost occupiable floor within the observation crown; not a Park Hyatt or Maybank space.
