## 0. Executive summary (for Claude)

This wedding is not a template job. Syed Saddiq & Bella Astillah are **national figures** — politics, entertainment, law, Sabah–Johor–KL geography, blended families (Ayden 9, Ara 6), and a **historic venue** (first reported akad nikah on Level 118, Menara Merdeka Maybank). The RSVP must feel **bespoke, editorial, mobile-native, bilingual (EN/BM minimum)**, and operationally bulletproof (multi-event RSVPs, VIP personalisation, WhatsApp-first sharing).

**What Malaysian "best" platforms do well:** personalised guest links, envelope/cover reveal, real-time RSVP dashboard, Waze/Maps, QR check-in, seating, digital angpow, guestbook, countdown, gallery, background music (user-initiated), CSV export.

### C3. UX patterns to **steal** (ethically — as inspiration)

1. **Single scroll, no tabs** — 2026 consensus: one vertical story (Private Wedding App, editorial reports)
2. **9:16 hero** — mobile-first; full viewport cover
3. **Quiet luxury palette** — 2–3 colours; for SSBA: white, warm ivory, **muted gold** (#B99A5B range), charcoal text — **not** royal blue Indian-template gold overload
4. **Islamic geometry as texture** — star-and-cross, mashrabiya, Merdeka 118 facets (we have `patterns.cjs`) at **5–15% opacity**
5. **Cinematic typography build** — names resolve over 2–3s on first open
6. **Smart RSVP branching** — attending yes → events → pax → dietary; no → graceful decline path
7. **Group links** — separate analytics for KL vs Muar vs Sabah circles
8. **"Contact tuan majlis"** — discreet WhatsApp to planner, not public phone spam

### C4. UX patterns to **avoid**

- Autoplay music / heavy video on cellular
- Stock "wedding couple silhouette" vectors
- 12-tab navigation (Home | Story | Events | Gallery | …)
- Pink glitter, excessive script fonts, Comic Sans BM
- Forcing email for RSVP (WhatsApp culture = phone primary)
- Public guest list or wish wall without moderation
- Treating political/legal saga as wedding "content"
- Misstating Bella's ethnicity or children's custody details
